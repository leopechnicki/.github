# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) Code of Conduct v2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards others

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at [leopsantos@hotmail.com](mailto:leopsantos@hotmail.com).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
